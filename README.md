# Engineering Forge

**Agentic Engineering Workbench — From engineering problems to proven solutions**

A Databricks-native, AppKit-based engineering environment in which qualified engineers direct specialized SI agents and deterministic engineering tools.

## Product direction
- Problem-first intake and source evidence, not predetermined manufacturing processes.
- Requirements and analysis of alternatives: reuse, source, repair, conventional manufacturing or AM.
- Real engineering artifacts, independent verification, revision-specific human decisions.
- Durable dependency-aware orchestration and a configuration-controlled digital thread.
- Governed knowledge reuse and measured outcomes.
